## Review Focus
1. Terminal fit and resize: no lost/garbled output when the panel opens, closes or is dragged; `pty.resize` settles once; no resize loop.
2. Privacy: the panel shows only redacted/basename data; no new data source.
3. Nothing typed; clipboard only; `isSessionId` before copy.
4. Closed panel costs nothing (no timers/polls); open panel refreshes history only on open/finish/refresh.
5. Layout: pins, sticky header, find bar, paste bar, pet, tool strip, title bar, focus mode still correct with the panel.
