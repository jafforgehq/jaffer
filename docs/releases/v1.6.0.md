## What is new in 1.6.0

**Voice, off by default: Claude reads its replies to you, and you can answer by speaking.**
- **Turn it on in Settings → Privacy and permissions.** Jaffer asks first, then downloads about 175 MB of speech models once.
- **`/voice` in the palette** starts and stops it for your terminal. When Claude finishes a turn, Jaffer reads the prose of its answer, then listens. What you say is typed into Claude's prompt, never with Enter. Esc cancels; Esc twice leaves.
- **Typed only when safe:** you are still in that terminal and Claude is idle. Text starting with `/`, `!` or `#`, or arriving while Claude is busy, is copied instead.
- **English only.** Speech takes a few seconds to start.
- **Privacy:** only the model download leaves your Mac. Audio and what you say are never saved or sent, and the window now refuses every network request of its own.
- **Jaffer is now GPL-3.0-or-later**, because the speech engine bundles espeak-ng.
- **The install is about 27 MB bigger**, with voice on or not.

Updating from 0.2.x or later asks first, as always. 0.1.x apps have no updater: install this version from this page once.
