## What is new in 1.5.0

**Several terminals are easier to live with: a Terminals list, a companion in each, zoom, and notifications that say which terminal.**
- **A Terminals section in the side panel.** Click a name to focus it, double-click to name it, drag to reorder, split from a row, zoom, close, or close all the idle ones at once.
- **A companion in every terminal**, and with two or more a coloured outline on the terminal where Claude works or needs you.
- **Zoom one terminal** with `⇧⌘Return`; the same key brings the grid back.
- **Notifications start with the terminal's folder**, and a click takes you to that terminal.

**Jaffer 1.5.0 makes the split view manageable.** It adds no setting, writes nothing new to disk, types nothing into a shell and changes no signing, entitlement or update setting. Still no tabs, no draggable divider, no fifth terminal and no saved layout. The notes of 1.4.2 and earlier still apply.

### What changed
- **The Terminals section** sits in the side panel between *Now* and *Past conversations*. One row per terminal: Claude's state, its name (your label, else its folder), and a second line (with the cost figure on, Claude's cost and context; otherwise what runs there, or *idle*). *New terminal* opens one (`⌘D` does the same). A row's *Split here* opens a new terminal in that row's folder, *Zoom* and the ✕ do what they say. Double-click a name or press `F2` to name a terminal (24 characters; the name lives only while Jaffer runs). Drag a row, or press `⌥↑` / `⌥↓`, to reorder: the grid and `⌘1`-`⌘4` follow. *Close idle…* asks once and ends every terminal where nothing runs, never the focused one; Cancel is the default.
- **A companion in each terminal.** The single mole or scene in the corner is gone; every terminal has its own, following only its own Claude and commands. The companion choice, size and on/off switch apply to all of them. With two or more terminals, a terminal where Claude works has an accent-coloured outline, and a warning-coloured one when it needs you; the pulse stops with Animations off and under macOS Reduce motion.
- **Zoom.** `⇧⌘Return` (also *Shell → Zoom Terminal*, the palette and a row's button) shows the focused terminal over the whole window. The others keep running but are hidden and are not resized. Focusing another terminal moves the zoom; splitting or closing one ends it.
- **Notifications name the terminal.** With two or more terminals, the "Claude needs you", "finished" and permission-request notifications start with the terminal's folder name. A click on one, or its *Show* button, brings the window forward and focuses that terminal. *Answer in terminal* in the panel or the title-bar chip also switches to the terminal that asks. The folder name is cut and redacted like any other text Jaffer shows, and kept in memory only.

### Limits
- The title-bar chip and the approval card in the panel still follow the focused terminal and do not say which terminal asks.
- Names and the order of terminals are not saved: the next start is one terminal, as before.
- Not tried on a real Mac: the `⇧⌘Return` keypress through the macOS menu, and a click on a notification focusing the right terminal (the page's side and the pure parts are tested; the Electron wiring in the main process is typechecked, not run).

Updating from 0.2.x or later asks first, as always. 0.1.x apps have no updater: install this version from this page once.
