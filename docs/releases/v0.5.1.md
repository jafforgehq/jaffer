## What is new in 0.5.1

**Resuming Claude is a click again.** After a restart, a crash or an update, the **Resume Claude** button appears in the title bar and one click takes the conversation back up. Jaffer no longer types `claude --resume` by itself: it was the only place where it wrote into your terminal without you, and every risky case found in 0.5.0 came from it. The one thing you give up: after a reboot with nobody at the Mac, the conversation now waits for your click.

**What stays from 0.5.0**
- **Restart Claude Code, to use an update** (command palette, or *Settings → Claude Code*). You confirm, your shell restarts in the same folder, and the conversation is taken up on the new version after a 3-second notice with a **Cancel**. It types only when the shell itself has the terminal, at an empty prompt, and never adds to a line you started typing.
- **Keep my session running in the background** (*Settings → Appearance*, off by default), **your Mac stays awake while Claude works**, the **notice for two Claude conversations**, *Quit and End Session* forgetting the conversation, and the question before turning the agent off while macOS runs your session.

**Changed**
- The setting *Resume Claude automatically* is gone. An old config file that still lists it is left alone and nothing reads it. *Offer to resume Claude Code* stays: it decides whether the conversation is remembered and the button offered.
- The crash retry, the limit of 3 attempts in 10 minutes, the give-up and its "Claude keeps stopping" notification are gone with it.
- The *Known limitation* listed for 0.5.0 (a program that forges the prompt marks could get the resume line added to a half-typed line) is gone: there is no automatic typing left for it to affect.

**Smaller fixes**
- The Resume button now shows with any shell, including `SHELL=/bin/sh` (which runs bash) and wrapper shells. It decides by which process has the terminal, not by the shell's name, and no longer calls `ps` over and over while a conversation is offered.
- `jaffer service remove` and `jaffer config set session.keepRunning false`, typed in the session that macOS itself runs, would end the terminal they were typed in: they now warn and need `--yes`. Turning the switch off or on by any route (Settings, `jaffer service`, `jaffer config set`) installs or removes the agent to match.
- A session started before the update that does not know a new feature says "Restart your session to use this" instead of "unknown method".
- Starting the daemon through macOS no longer fails when it lands while the old daemon is still exiting (launchd's throttle): it asks again.
- Settings refreshes the agent's status after a Cancel; *Quit and End Session* waits at most 5 seconds for each of its two steps; the first-run switch says the agent is active from the next login or restart.
- The hold that keeps the Mac awake is taken up again if `caffeinate` ends by itself while Claude works.
- A long command behind `sudo`, `env`, `nice`, `time` or `nohup` (`sudo --user deploy vim`) is recognised as an interactive program and does not keep the Mac awake by itself.

**Updating with a session running (a drag-in install)**
If you replace Jaffer.app by dragging it in while a session runs, that session keeps its 0.5.0 daemon until it restarts, and until then it behaves like 0.5.0: it still resumes Claude by itself, still gives up after 3 attempts, and a retry notice may read "in 120 s". The new app shows no "Claude keeps stopping" notification for it. To get the new daemon: choose *Quit and End Session*, or restart the session (an update from the app does this itself, after asking).

**By hand, on your Mac**
The launchd and power parts are tested against stand-ins, because the real ones touch your LaunchAgents and power settings. Not yet checked on a real Mac, and not verified until the owner has checked them there: a real `launchctl bootstrap` and bootout of the generated plist, launchd restarting a killed daemon in about 5 seconds with the screen back and the **Resume Claude** button offered, `kickstart` on a running job and during launchd's throttle, turning the switch off from inside the job it ends, *Login Items → Allow in the Background* showing the agent as Jaffer, `pmset -g assertions` showing the hold while Claude works and not after, a closed lid with and without power and a display, logging out and in, a reboot, an update with the agent on, a real `claude --resume` of a real conversation (the button and *Restart Claude Code*), and a desktop notification.

Updating from 0.2.x or later asks first, as always. 0.1.x apps have no updater: install this version from this page once.
