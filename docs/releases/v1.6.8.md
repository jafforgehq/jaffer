## What is new in 1.6.8

**A lighter pulse on the terminal outline.** With two or more terminals, the outline of a terminal whose Claude is working or waiting for you now fades in and out without repainting the terminal, so it costs less while Claude works.
- **The Terminals row keeps its second line readable.** The row's buttons now sit on the name's line, so the cost and context figures under it are no longer covered when you point at a row.

**Jaffer 1.6.8 is a small polish release.** The outline looks the same as before and still stops with Reduce motion or Animations off. Nothing is typed, sent or written, there is no new setting, and no signing, entitlement or update setting changes. The notes of 1.6.4 and earlier still apply.

Updating from 0.2.x or later asks first, as always. 0.1.x apps have no updater: install this version from this page once.
