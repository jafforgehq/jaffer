## What is new in 1.6.6

**Resume a past conversation while Claude is running.** Click a past conversation while Claude waits at its prompt, and a question appears under the terminal's chip: *End the conversation running here and resume it?*
- **End and resume** types `/exit` into that terminal, waits until the shell is free, then resumes the one you picked. The ended conversation is in your past conversations.
- **Cancel** (the default) types nothing. While Claude is working or waiting for a permission answer, nothing changes: the command is copied, as before.

<!-- card-end -->

**Jaffer 1.6.6 adds one fixed line that Jaffer may type into a running Claude: `/exit`.** It is typed only after your click on *End and resume*, only into the terminal the question was asked in, only while that terminal's Claude is idle at its prompt (its hooks say so) and only while *Settings → Privacy and permissions → Resume typing* is on. If the shell is not free within 10 seconds, nothing more is typed and the resume command is copied. Half-typed text in Claude's prompt is cleared first (Ctrl-E, Ctrl-U); not checked against every Claude Code version. No new setting, nothing is stored, and no signing, entitlement or update setting changes. The notes of 1.6.5 and earlier still apply.

Updating from 0.2.x or later asks first, as always. 0.1.x apps have no updater: install this version from this page once.
